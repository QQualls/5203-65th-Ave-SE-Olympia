About This Home

Perfectly positioned along the 14th hole near the green at Capitol City Golf Club, this beautifully maintained rambler offers immersive golf course views paired with comfortable everyday living. Featuring 3 bedrooms, 1.75 baths, and 1,502 SF, the home showcases tasteful updates throughout including a beautifully remodeled kitchen and updated primary bath. Vaulted ceilings and a striking fireplace create an inviting living space that flows seamlessly into the formal dining area. The open kitchen connects naturally to the family room with French door access to the back patio. Improvements include a newer roof, updated flooring, and fresh interior and exterior paint. Complete with a spacious 2-car garage, mature landscaping, and established fruit trees.
